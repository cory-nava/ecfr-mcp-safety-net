## Reading

The naive "BM25-first" recommendation lost to the data: RRF fusion never
beats vectors + deterministic citation lookup on any k, and BM25 alone is
weak everywhere except exact citations (where the citation fast-path is
strictly better — 1.00 by construction, and correct by design: a query
that IS a citation is a lookup, not retrieval). The BM25 index stays built
in `meta.sqlite` as a fallback/debug leg, but it is not in the ranking
path. The remaining headroom is paraphrase queries (r@1 0.417) — the honest
next lever, not more fusion tuning on n=50.

## Round 4 — retrieval experiments (same frozen set)

Motivation: round-2 recall (0.64/0.78/0.84) left headroom. Literature
(2026) points to cross-encoder reranking and chunking as the two
highest-EV moves; measured here first that 39.7% of corpus docs exceed
bge's 512-token limit and are silently truncated.

| system | r@1 | r@3 | r@5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| whole-section + citation (production) | 0.640 | 0.780 | 0.840 |
| chunked, best-chunk + citation | 0.620 | 0.800 | 0.860 |
| rerank top-50 sections (bge-reranker-base) | 0.520 | 0.700 | 0.740 |
| rerank top-50 chunks (bge-reranker-base) | 0.520 | 0.700 | 0.760 |
| BM25 alone | 0.280 | 0.440 | 0.540 |

Findings:
- **Chunking is a wash** (450-token windows, 64 overlap, title prepended;
  6,222 chunks; section = best chunk): -0.02 r@1, +0.02 r@3/r@5 vs
  whole-section. Fixes truncation in principle; best-chunk aggregation
  dilutes topical signal on paraphrase queries (r@1 0.417 -> 0.250).
- **bge-reranker-base hurts in both configurations** (sections and chunks):
  0.52/0.70/0.74-0.76. The truncation hypothesis (reranker only saw the
  first ~500 tokens) was tested by reranking chunks instead — same
  result, so truncation is not the cause. Likely MS MARCO training
  misaligned with regulatory text (rewards surface lexical overlap;
  promotes distractor docs like the OMB control-number list that cites
  everything).
- Production system unchanged: citation fast-path + whole-section vectors.

Deferred, not disproven: bge-reranker-v2-m3 (2026 self-host default;
heavier), HyDE / LLM query expansion (needs OpenRouter spend approval;
literature reports ~+1pp and active harm on exact-identifier queries —
test late), larger embedding model (literature: +1.8pp for 2.4x cost).
